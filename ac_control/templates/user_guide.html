User Guide

AC Control Center turns classroom door sensors and IR blasters into a school-wide energy policy. An ESP32 in each room reports state; a central Raspberry Pi decides when the AC may run.

Accounts
  • Room accounts log in with username/password or room number + PIN.
  • Admins are created with the CLI, never from the public register form.
Room dashboard
  • Temperatures are stored in Celsius and shown in Fahrenheit.
  • Setpoint is the last temperature sent to the AC. Turning the unit on (remote or dashboard) uses that value.
  • Auto-shutoff queues a POWER_OFF when the door stays open. Buzz can remind the room until the door closes.
  • Force ON/OFF buttons queue commands; they run on the next ESP32 heartbeat.
  • A stale badge means the classroom ESP32 has not checked in recently.
