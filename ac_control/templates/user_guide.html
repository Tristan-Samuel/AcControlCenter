User Guide

AC Control Center turns classroom window sensors and IR blasters into a school-wide energy policy. A Raspberry Pi in each room reports temperature and window state; a central Pi decides when the AC may run.

Accounts
  • Room accounts log in with username/password or room number + PIN.
  • Admins are created with the CLI, never from the public register form.
Room dashboard
  • Temperatures are stored in Celsius and shown in Fahrenheit.
  • Auto-shutoff queues a POWER_OFF for your room Pi when a window stays open.
  • Force ON/OFF buttons queue commands; they run on the next Pi heartbeat.
  • A stale badge means the classroom Pi has not checked in recently.
